How this lab works
Detailed technical scope below is provided in its original English. Interface translations do not change chemical data.
Exhaustive within a defined model
Every unique connected, neutral, closed-shell bonding graph for supported formulas. Carbon has valence 4, nitrogen 3, oxygen 2, and F, Cl, Br and I valence 1. Rings, branching and multiple bonds are included. Unstable or highly strained candidates remain available but receive lower priority when peroxide, enol, strained-ring or other flagged motifs are detected. The score is a structural heuristic, not a measurement or reliable prediction of stability.
Small molecules, clear limits
Formula searches support 12 heavy atoms at DBE 0, 10 at DBE 1, 9 at DBE 2, and 7 at DBE 3–4. Offline structure viewing supports 64 heavy atoms; the RDKit service accepts 256 base atoms, up to 64 stereo results and 50 requested conformers per calculation. Each ring or double bond adds one DBE; a triple bond adds two. Searches stop at 22 seconds or 12,000 candidates with an explanation, never a partial list labeled complete. Imported structures are not an exhaustive isomer search.
Configuration and conformation
The RDKit workspace separates supported R/S and E/Z stereoisomers and samples conformers without mixing configurations. Charges, radicals, isotopes and salts are supported where the toolkit and selected format can preserve them. The neutral-only and explicit-Kekulé restrictions apply to the offline compatibility parser, not the RDKit service. Unsupported chemical annotations are reported rather than silently discarded. Primary halogenoalkanes include methyl halides here. Legacy coordinates remain illustrative; the RDKit view uses the selected stereo-verified conformer and reports its geometry method. Qualitative π clouds are separate from imported quantum grids.

Bring a structure in
Paste SMILES, MOL/SDF V2000, or Orgchem Visualizer JSON, or choose a file. Connectivity and bond orders are preserved; coordinates are regenerated. Toolkit imports support up to 64 records; the legacy neutral importer supports 500. Files must fit the 1 MB input budget. The RDKit server preserves supported stereo, charge, radical and isotope annotations. Unsupported features are explained rather than silently removed.
Files can also be dropped onto the molecule viewer.
